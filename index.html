Numerical method and interpretation

The stochastic process uses the exact direct Gillespie algorithm for birth and death events, with competition divided by K. Each initial individual is sampled independently from the normalized shape of f₀, and N₀ = ⌊K ∫ f₀⌋. No mutations are introduced. The deterministic equation ∂ₜf = (a − ∫cf)f is discretized by midpoint quadrature in trait space (cell averages for custom initial shapes) and advanced by a positivity-preserving exponential midpoint method.

The reference ESD is Aδ₀ for 0 < A ≤ 1, and two symmetric atoms for 1 < A < 2. The displayed energy is ½∫∫c(x,y)ν(dx)ν(dy) − ∫a(x)ν(dx) + ½‖ξ̄‖²c. The Gaussian convolution is for display only.

This is an exploratory implementation in JavaScript, not the archived Python solver. Large article presets may be slow; use Stop to cancel. The energy, mass and diversity series are recorded more frequently than the snapshots for long simulations. Deterministic discretization errors can be checked by increasing the grid size and reducing the time step. Random seeds reproduce runs within this browser implementation, not the original Python trajectories.
